Common questions

Which inspection should I request?

Select the inspection you believe you need, or choose “Not sure.” We will review your property information and discuss the appropriate scope before scheduling.

Are lab results immediate?

No. Where laboratory sampling is required, timing depends on sample delivery and laboratory processing.

Can I request multiple units?

Yes. Include the number of units and any relevant property details in the request form so we can quote the scope accurately.

Is a certificate guaranteed?

No. Any certificate depends on the inspection findings and applicable requirements. We will explain the next steps if the property does not meet the relevant criteria.

Inspection requirements can vary by property and certification type. Final scope, sampling and documentation requirements are confirmed before service.
