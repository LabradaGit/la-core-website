RESIDENTIAL RENOVATION • BALTIMORE, MARYLAND

North Kenwood Avenue

Interior Renovation & Property Improvements

THE PROJECT

Repairs that became a complete transformation.

North Kenwood Avenue combined corrective repairs with interior and exterior improvements. The work included rebuilding deteriorated exterior stairs, repairing a fence gate, correcting damaged and uneven subfloor areas, and preparing the main level for new flooring.

Inside, the renovation progressed through LVP installation and finish work before culminating in a dramatically refreshed basement with new carpet, clean gray walls, bright white trim and updated lighting.

THE WORK BEHIND THE FINISH

Fixing the structure before covering it.

Flooring work began with the conditions underneath it. Damaged areas were opened, repaired and checked for level before new material went down. The project photos document that progression from subfloor correction through LVP installation and final interior finishes.

Outside, deteriorated stair components were removed and rebuilt, while the fence gate received reinforcement and repair.
